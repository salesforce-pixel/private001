✓ Discharged home 18 July — infection settled; two lab questions stay open

She was discharged home on 18 July after 11 days. She has been fever-free since about 15 July, and the pre-discharge blood tests reversed almost everything that looked wrong two days earlier — but her bile-duct enzymes are still climbing and a clotting test was never repeated.

Dr. Hingmani was inpatient at ILBS Delhi from 8 to 18 July with acute cholangitis (a bile-duct infection) on a re-blocked left bile segment. After a breakthrough fever on 14 July cancelled a first discharge attempt, her left biliary drain was exchanged and repositioned — and that worked. The 14 July fever was her last one. The 18 July bloods show the infection response fully settled: bilirubin back to normal, white cells normal, and the chemotherapy-related platelet drop has recovered (85 → 175).

Two things did not resolve. Her bile-duct enzymes (ALP and GGT) kept rising even as bilirubin normalised — a pattern that can point to a bile segment that is still partly blocked, and the one trend to watch at the follow-up visit. And her clotting test (INR 1.41) was never repeated before she left, so it is unresolved. Detail sits in Labs and Care plan.

On chemotherapy: the C3D8 dose (gemcitabine + cisplatin + durvalumab #3) was never given. No chemotherapy since C3D1 on 6 July — 12 days. Restarting is now an outpatient decision, not a scheduled date. The blood-count barrier has cleared (platelets 175, above the usual threshold); the discharge note also puts biliary stenting on the outpatient agenda for the first time.
